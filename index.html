Obi-Wan Lightsaber Hilt and Electronics System

In progress, June 2026

Overview

I designed an Obi-Wan-style lightsaber hilt with a 1.2-inch hollow electronics chamber by modeling modular sections in FreeCAD. The project combines CAD modeling, 3D-print preparation, and internal electronics planning.

Focus: modular hilt design, connector tolerances, electronics layout, and Creality K2 print readiness.

What I Built

  • Designed a hilt with a 1.2-inch hollow electronics chamber by modeling modular sections in FreeCAD
  • Prepared the model for Creality K2 printing by splitting the hilt into printable modules
  • Adjusted connector tolerances for print fit and assembly
  • Planned the internal layout for an Arduino Nano, LED strips, buttons, and wiring
  • Accounted for soldering access, electronics clearance, and hardware fit constraints
